# Webtop Smartschool modules

Reconfirm the live page, student, study year, period and date filters on each run. Use visible navigation and report coverage limits.

## Homework

Student Card 11. Read the homework column, not lesson-topic text. Preserve dates, subjects, teachers, lesson numbers, exact text, page ranges, deadlines and attachment names. Dates are lesson dates.

## Timetable

Student Card 10 provides the current weekday and lesson grid. Timetable_Changes may require permission. Do not infer historical changes from the current grid.

## Grades

Student Card 6 exposes study period and assessment rows. Preserve event, teacher, subject, grade, verbal evaluation, weight, components, notes and date. Missing grade is not zero. Do not select retest controls while reading.

## Events and attendance

Student Card 4 exposes event type, date, lesson, study group, note and justification. A missing presence row is not proof of absence. Student Card 5 contains outside-lesson events.

## Messages

Messages inbox exposes sender, date/time, subject and body. Filter by sent date, summarize explicit deadlines and attachments. Reading is allowed; replies, deletion, moves and bulk read-state changes are not.

Keep module reports separate and do not feed grades, events or messages into homework change detection.
